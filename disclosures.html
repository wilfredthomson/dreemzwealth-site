5. Regular Plans and how we are paid

  • Every investment made through us is in the Regular Plan of the scheme.
  • The Asset Management Company pays a commission on your investment. This is a trail commission, plus any other commission that SEBI permits, and it is paid out of the scheme's expense ratio.
  • So a Regular Plan has a higher expense ratio than the Direct Plan of the same scheme.
  • You can invest in the Direct Plan yourself. There is no distributor commission in a Direct Plan, but we cannot service Direct Plan investments.
  • Before you invest, we will tell you the commission we receive on each scheme we suggest. Our current commission rates are determined by Wealthy platform on a monthly basis and can be made available from Wealthy platform upon request.

6. Conflicts of interest

Commission rates differ between schemes and between AMCs. We recommend schemes on the basis of suitability for you, not the commission we receive. We disclose our commission so that you can judge this for yourself.

7. Scheme documents and risk

Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.

  • The value of your investment can go up or down.
  • Past performance may or may not be sustained in future and is not a guarantee of future returns.
  • Before investing, read the Scheme Information Document (SID), the Statement of Additional Information (SAI) and the Key Information Memorandum (KIM). They are available on each AMC's website and at www.amfiindia.com.

8. Calculators

The calculators on this website use assumed rates of return and inflation for illustration only. They are not a recommendation of any scheme and do not promise any return.

9. NRI investors

  • NRI investments in mutual funds are governed by FEMA, the Income Tax Act, SEBI regulations and each AMC's own rules.
  • They are also governed by the laws of the country where you live.
  • Some AMCs do not accept investments from residents of certain countries.
  • It is your responsibility to check that investing is permitted where you live. Please confirm the tax treatment with a Chartered Accountant.
  • Our UAE contact number is provided for the convenience of Indian NRIs; we distribute only Indian mutual fund schemes.

10. Complaints and grievances

  1. Write to wilfred@dreemzwealth.com or call +91 94462 06915. We will acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 7 working days.
  2. If it is not resolved, contact the investor service team of the AMC concerned. Their contact details are in the scheme documents and on the AMC's website.
  3. If you are still not satisfied, you can lodge a complaint on SEBI SCORES (scores.sebi.gov.in) or use the Online Dispute Resolution portal (smartodr.in).

11. Privacy

  • What we collect: when you send the contact form, we collect your name, mobile number, message and the page you sent it from.
  • Where it is stored: in a Google Sheet and in an email alert. Only Wilfred Thomson can access them.
  • How we use it: only to reply to your enquiry. We do not sell or share your details, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you separately agree to receive them.
  • How long we keep it: enquiry records are kept for 24 months and then deleted.
  • Deleting your details: email wilfred@dreemzwealth.com at any time to ask us to delete them.
  • Clients: client records are kept confidential and are retained as required by AMFI and SEBI rules.
