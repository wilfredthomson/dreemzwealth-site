Our method

Four steps from a dream to a portfolio

STEP 1

Dream

We start with your life goals: a child's education, a home, a comfortable retirement, a return to India.

STEP 2

Plan

Each goal gets an amount, a timeline and a risk level that suits you and your family.

STEP 3

Invest

We build a mutual fund portfolio for each goal and set up SIPs or lump-sum investments.

STEP 4

Grow

We review your portfolio regularly and rebalance as markets and your life change.
