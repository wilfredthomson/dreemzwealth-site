How we work

Requirements first, then the right mutual fund portfolio

A clear, six-step process that starts with your goals and continues for as long as you stay invested.

  1. Discovery conversation

    An introductory meeting in person, by phone or on video. We talk about your family, your income, your existing investments and what you want your money to do for you.

  2. Risk profiling

    A structured questionnaire to understand how much risk you are able and willing to take, and how much time you have for each goal.

  3. Goal mapping

    Each goal is written down with a target amount, a date and a priority, adjusted for inflation so the plan reflects real future costs.

  4. Portfolio recommendation

    We propose an asset allocation and suitable mutual fund schemes for each goal, and explain why each was chosen in plain language, along with the risks.

  5. Onboarding and investment

    We help you complete KYC (and FATCA declarations for NRIs), then set up your SIPs or lump-sum investments. Every transaction happens only with your consent.

  6. Ongoing reviews

    We review your portfolio periodically and whenever your life changes, rebalancing to keep each goal on track.
