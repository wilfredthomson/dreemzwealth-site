Services

Wealth creation and family protection

We help families in India and NRIs abroad turn life goals into disciplined mutual fund investment plans, with honesty, transparency and long-term care.

What we help you achieve

Solutions for each stage of life

NRI Investments in India

Invest in Indian mutual funds from abroad through NRE and NRO routes, with KYC and FATCA compliance in order. Investments are structured in line with current NRI tax rules and coordinated with your Chartered Accountant.

Goal-based Mutual Fund Investing

A child's education, a home, a wedding, wealth creation: each goal gets its own timeline, risk level and fund mix.

Retirement Investment Planning

Build your retirement corpus steadily, then plan a reliable income stream from it.

Returning to India

Moving back takes planning years ahead. We help you transition your investments, update your residential status with fund houses, and align your portfolio with your new life, working alongside your CA on the tax side.

Family Protection Review

We assess how much life and health cover your family needs and identify the gaps, so your investment plan is not derailed by the unexpected.
