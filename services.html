Services

Mutual fund investing for every life goal

We help families in India and NRIs abroad turn life goals into disciplined mutual fund investment plans, with honesty, transparency and long-term care.

What we help you achieve

Mutual fund investing for each stage of life

NRI Investments in India

Invest in Indian mutual funds from abroad through NRE and NRO routes, with KYC and FATCA compliance in order. Investments are structured in line with current NRI tax rules and coordinated with your Chartered Accountant.

Goal-based Mutual Fund Investing

A child's education, a home, a wedding: each goal gets its own timeline, risk level and fund mix.

Retirement Investing

Build a retirement corpus through mutual funds, and later draw regular withdrawals from it through a Systematic Withdrawal Plan.

Returning to India

Moving back takes planning years ahead. We help you transition your investments, update your residential status with fund houses, and align your portfolio with your new life, working alongside your CA on the tax side.

How we deliver it

Trusted, transparent offerings

Mutual Funds

Mutual Fund Investments

New portfolio creation, review of your existing mutual fund holdings when you ask us to, and rebalancing where suitable.

SIP

SIP Investments

Systematic Investment Plans (monthly, weekly or daily) to invest regularly and with discipline.

SWP

Regular Withdrawals (SWP)

Systematic Withdrawal Plans that pay out an amount you choose, monthly or quarterly. Withdrawals reduce your units, and the value of the remaining investment can go down.
